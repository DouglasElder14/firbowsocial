Reporting
Numbers, numbers, numbers! I love seeing the stories that data tells — showing what content works, why it works, and how that can shape what you do next. When you know what your audience has done, you can start to predict what they'll do next.
Content Creation
Sometimes you just have to get stuck in! I love creating content that your audience will actually enjoy — using the data to find the right formats and the right messages. From entertainment to investing, I can fit the content to the audience.
Paid Social
Social media has become critical for advertising — which makes it more important than ever to get your paid strategy right. I work with you to make sure we reach the right people in the right place at the right time. And don't worry — you'll be surprised how far your money can go on Social!
Channel Set-Up
Mighty oaks from tiny acorns grow. Creating new channels can feel daunting with dozens of things to do — but I love starting the journey. I've set up Instagram, LinkedIn, X and YouTube channels across multiple industries, and I'll make sure you get it right from day one.

Strategy
I always ask questions first. I want to understand your business, your goals and dreams, and what keeps you up at night. Big picture first! From there I can help build a solid Social Media and/or Influencer strategy — whether it's focused on sales, brand awareness, or both. (I love a funnel!)
Training
Upskilling others and getting them confident with social really excites me. If I'm successful, you might never need my help ever again — and honestly, I'd wear that as a badge of honour. One-on-one, group sessions, or helpful documents — I've done it all and it's always a success.
Ideas ideas ideas
Once something takes root in my brain — your business, your goals, a new product — ideas pop up relentlessly. Even at 2am! I'd love to lend that superpower to whatever you're working towards, and help find the best and most effective ones for you.
Planning
I love turning breezy ideas into concrete plans and easy-to-deliver schedules. Big or small — I'll plan the phasing for one campaign or give you a full roadmap for the year ahead.
Consultancy
Before you invest in the people or the output — let's chat. Setting things up with the right goals, building a solid strategy, or finding the best people can stop you wasting money doing the wrong thing. I'll always give you my most honest guidance, including if I don't think you need social media at all.
Recruitment
I've helped build efficient in-house social media teams that save businesses the cost of agencies. I've recruited for many Social Media and Influencer roles, and can help with designing team structures, writing job specs, designing and conducting interviews, and helping with hiring decisions.
